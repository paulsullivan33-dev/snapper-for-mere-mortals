# t01 — what got installed, and how this build of snapper is compiled

The sandbox apt frontend lock was held by another process for the whole
session, so the packages were fetched with apt-get download and unpacked
with dpkg-deb -x. These are the unmodified Ubuntu 24.04 archive files.

$ apt-cache policy snapper btrfs-progs | grep -A1 ^[a-z]
snapper:
  Installed: (none)
--
btrfs-progs:
  Installed: (none)

$ snapper --version
snapper 0.10.6
libsnapper 7.2.0
flags btrfs,lvm,no-ext4,xattrs,rollback,btrfs-quota,no-selinux

$ btrfs --version
btrfs-progs v6.6.3
$ ls snapper-tool/debs/   (the packages, exactly as the archive serves them)
btrfs-progs_6.6.3-1.1build2_amd64.deb
libbtrfs0t64_6.6.3-1.1build2_amd64.deb
libsnapper7t64_0.10.6-1.1build1_amd64.deb
snapper_0.10.6-1.1build1_amd64.deb
